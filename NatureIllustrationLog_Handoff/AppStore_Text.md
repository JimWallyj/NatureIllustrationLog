## Description (max 4000)
Nature Illustration Log keeps everything for your next nature illustration in one place: photos of the subject, where you found it, what you noticed in the field, and your plans for the artwork — then photos of the finished piece.

MADE FOR THE FIELD
• Take a photo with the camera, or add photos from your Photos library or Files
• Each photo keeps the date it was taken
• Pin the exact spot on a map — tap the location arrow to drop the pin where you're standing
• Write field notes while the details are fresh

PLAN AND FINISH YOUR ARTWORK
• Keep notes and plans for the illustration project
• Add photos of your sketches and finished illustrations in their own album
• Browse each album, zoom into photos, and choose which photo leads the file

IPHONE, IPAD, AND MAC
• Works on iPhone, iPad, and Mac — buy once, use on all three. No subscription. No in-app purchases.
• Send files between your own devices with AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or Files; they arrive ready to edit
• Updating a file from another device keeps the older version for 30 days, just in case
• Short on iPhone space? Send files to your Mac and keep a lightweight copy on the iPhone
• Share a read-only copy with a friend who also has the app

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your photos, notes, and locations stay on your device unless you choose to send them

The illustration itself is made with your own tools — this app is your reference log and project notebook.
