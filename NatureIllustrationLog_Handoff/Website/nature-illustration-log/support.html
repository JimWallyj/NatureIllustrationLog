Common questions

How do I add photos?

In Add File, on a file's screen, or with + in an album, choose Take Photo (iPhone and iPad), Photos Library, or Files. Photos from Photos Library and Files can be chosen several at a time.

How do I record where I found a subject?

Tap Record Photo Location (or Edit Location), then tap the map where you found it. If you're standing there now, tap the location arrow in the map's lower-right corner. Tap Done to save.

How do I move files between my iPhone, iPad, and Mac?

In the file list, tap Select, check the files, tap the Share icon, and choose Send to My Devices. Send them with AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or Files, and open them on the other device. On the Mac you can also use Save to a Folder…. Files don't sync automatically.

Why wasn't I offered "Update This File"?

Update appears only when the incoming file is the same file sent from your own device with Send to My Devices, and the copy on the device isn't read-only. Two files that were created separately — even with the same name — are kept separate, so nothing is ever overwritten by mistake. A replaced version is kept for 30 days in Settings → Recently Replaced.

How do I share a file with someone else?

Choose Share Read-Only. The recipient needs Nature Illustration Log to open it, and can view and delete it but not edit or pass it on.

The camera, Photos, or location doesn't work

Check the app's permissions in your device's Settings (on the Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security). The location arrow may not find you indoors or under heavy cover — tap the map to drop the pin instead.

My iPhone is running out of space

Use Send to Mac and Free Up Space to keep the full photos on your Mac and a lightweight copy on the iPhone, or Settings → Optimize Photo Storage to shrink photos already in the app.
