Privacy Policy

Nature Illustration Log · Effective November 10, 2026

In short: Nature Illustration Log does not collect, store, or share any of your information. Your photos, notes, and locations stay on your device unless you choose to send a file to someone.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The developer never receives your photos, notes, locations, or any information about how you use the app.

Where your information is kept

Everything you add to the app — photos, dates, field notes, illustration notes, and map pins — is stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Deleting a file in the app, or deleting the app, removes it from that device.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos: to add photos you choose and read the date they were taken. When you take a photo with the app's camera, the app can also save the full-quality original to your Photos library ("Add Photos Only").
  • Camera (iPhone and iPad): to take photos of nature subjects and illustrations.
  • Location (while using the app): to show where you are on the map and, when you tap the location arrow, to place a pin at your current location.

Each permission is optional. You can change them at any time in your device's Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Maps

The map, place search, and place names use Apple Maps. When you search for a place or drop a pin, the search text or pin location is sent to Apple to show the map and look up the place name. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; the developer does not receive it.

Sending files

A file leaves your device only when you choose to send it — for example with AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or Files. You decide who receives it. A file shared "read-only" can't be edited in the app, but it is not encrypted, so share only with people you trust.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
